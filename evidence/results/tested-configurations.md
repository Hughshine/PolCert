## Remaining Semantic Gaps

| Request | Reason | Required work |
|---|---|---|
| `symbolic or runtime-dependent stride steps` | The supported front-end stride form requires a nonzero integer literal. This keeps the lowered candidate domain affine and avoids adding a runtime proof obligation for the step sign and nonzero-ness. | Add a sign/nonzero certificate or checked precondition for affine/dynamic step expressions, then reuse the LoopStride affine-guard lowering under that certificate. |
| `full --candldep --scalpriv` | Current support is conservative oracle tuning; schedules that rely on privatized scalar instances need a checked storage rewrite. | Materialize private scalar storage or an equivalent loop-local representation and prove preservation under the scalar-privatization precondition. |
