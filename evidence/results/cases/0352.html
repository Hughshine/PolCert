No optimizer candidate; Pluto refused its illegal final schedule

What This Case Checks

Runs the diamond-tiling producer and classifies the resulting transformation or producer failure.

Expected outcome
The fixed Pluto producer must refuse the illegal final schedule, emit no candidate program, and return exit code 1.
Recorded outcome
Pluto rejected its illegal final schedule, returned 1, and produced no final candidate for PolCert.
Why this test matters
The suite distinguishes a verified diamond proposal from inputs for which Pluto produces no usable proposal.

Why Rejection Is Correct

Classification
Illegal final schedule rejected by the producer
Reason
Pluto detects that its final diamond schedule is illegal and refuses to emit it. This is producer-side rejection, not a PolCert validator failure.
