What This Case Checks

Checks the complete two-level tiling route, including the requested downstream loop annotation.

Expected outcome
The two-level tiling producer must be accepted, while the uncertifiable innermost parallel annotation is skipped.
Recorded outcome
Two-level tiling was accepted and differs from one-level output; the uncertifiable innermost parallel annotation was not emitted.
Verdict
PASS means the verified part was retained and the uncertifiable optional effect was not emitted.
Why this test matters
Composition is sound only when both tiling levels and the optional consumer are independently accepted.
