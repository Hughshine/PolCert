What This Case Checks

Checks the complete two-level tiling route, including the requested downstream loop annotation.

Expected outcome
The permutable-band validator must accept the component without emitting a final Loop, with no fallback or rejection alarm.
Recorded outcome
Two-level diamond producer and consumer contracts matched.
Verdict
PASS means the recorded behavior matched the expected result for this case.
Why this test matters
Composition is sound only when both tiling levels and the optional consumer are independently accepted.
