reject-isldep-candldep

Driver option request rejected; no transformation applied

What This Case Checks

Exercises one Pluto-compatible driver option combination and its declared route or effect contract.

Expected outcome
The driver must reject this option request for its declared reason and apply no transformation.
Recorded outcome
Unsupported route rejected as declared.
Why this test matters
The wrapper must reject contradictory or unsupported requests and route accepted requests without silently dropping required effects.

Why Rejection Is Correct

Classification

Term meanings

  • effects: number of structural effect conditions declared by this driver case.
  • tiling-route=none: this case does not invoke the specialized tiling validator.
