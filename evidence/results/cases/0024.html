gemver

Original and optimized affine schedules mutually refine

What This Case Checks

Checks the source schedule against the optimizer schedule in both refinement directions.

Expected outcome
Both source-to-optimized and optimized-to-source refinement checks must succeed.
Recorded outcome
Schedules are mutually refining.
Verdict
PASS means the recorded behavior matched the expected result for this case.
Why this test matters
A schedule change is usable only when it preserves the source program's behavior.
