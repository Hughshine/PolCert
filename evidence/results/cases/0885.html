What This Case Checks

Injects one declared fault into a tiling, affine, or consumer stage and checks the exact failure boundary.

Expected outcome
The tiling stage must remain accepted, while the uncertifiable optional annotation is omitted.
Recorded outcome
The route stopped at the declared failing stage and emitted no unchecked continuation.
Verdict
PASS means the verified part was retained and the uncertifiable optional effect was not emitted.
Why this test matters
A composed route must stop at the first uncertified stage and must never emit an unchecked program.
