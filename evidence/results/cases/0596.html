matmul-init

Two-level tiling

What This Case Checks

Checks output agreement and the additional loop hierarchy produced by two-level tiling.

Expected outcome
Baseline and generated executions must produce the same output.
Recorded outcome
The source and optimized programs produced the same result. The optimized program shows two-level tiling.
Why this test matters
This distinguishes a working two-level route from a successful fallback to a simpler program.
